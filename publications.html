8.- Gefaell J, Vigo R, Rolán-Alvarez E. 2025. Does differential predation of Littorina saxatilis colour morphs contribute to the maintenance of a colour cline? Insights from a field tethering experiment. Journal of Molluscan Studies, 91: eyae057. https://academic.oup.com/mollus/article/91/1/eyae057/8058467 [pdf]

In this paper, we describe a field experiment aimed at testing selection for crypsis by crabs as a mechanism maintaining the color monomorphism seen in L. saxatilis populations from wave-exposed localities. We corroborate that the local morph (lineata) is more cryptic than alternative morphs against the local background rocks, but the results of the tethering experiment yielded only partial support for selection for crypsis.

7.- Lau SLY, Gefaell J, Williams G, Rolán-Alvarez E. 2025. The role of contextual and individual factors in male mate choice for size in a marine snail. Animal Behaviour, 220: 123043. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anbehav.2024.11.021 [pdf]

In this paper, we describe an experiment aimed at assessing the repeatability of male mate choice for size in Echinolittorina malaccana, as well as at determining to what extent the strength of this phenomenon varies depending on contextual factors. We found that repeatability, although consistent across scenarios, was higher when the females involved in the experiment were the same individuals, suggesting that imprinting or female quality might play an important role in this behavior.

2.- Torres-Muros L, Sánchez-Robles JM, Pimentel A, Moscoso B, Bugallo A, Achig L, Gonzalez I, Hernández LE, Gijón J, Botella M, Linares D, Linares M, Gefaell J, Rolán-Alvarez E. 2025. Acceptance of evolutionary theory among pre-service teacher students and in-service teachers in Ecuador. Evolution: Education and Outreach, 18: 3. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12052-025-00219-2 [pdf]

In this paper, we explore the knowledge and acceptance of evolutionary theory in pre-service teacher students and in-service teachers from two regions of Ecuador.
